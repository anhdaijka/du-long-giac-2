# Worked Branch Matrix

Status: reconstructed source model plus proposed adaptation policy; not Story Skills canon.

## Branch point

| Branch | Trigger | Source | Exclusive material | Converges |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lien route | `choice=LIEN` | T110 / D010 | broken-seal investigation; Lien direct-rider claim | T130 |
| Vu route | `choice=VU` | T120 / D020 | dispatch ledger; third-seal evidence | T130 |

## Irreversible differences

- One player cannot complete T110 and T120 under the source branch model.
- Route knowledge differs before T130.
- Captain Lien's statements differ by route context and must not be flattened into unconditional fact.

## Proposed adaptation policy

**Parallel protagonists**.

- Protagonist A follows Lien's route.
- Protagonist B follows Vu's route.
- Their stories unfold in parallel before both reach Stone Gate Pass through different causal chains.
- The novel preserves each route's exclusive knowledge rather than assigning both routes to one protagonist.

## Convergence bridge required

The game simply routes both branches to T130. The novel needs a NOVELIZATION BRIDGE explaining:
- why both protagonists arrive at Stone Gate Pass in the same narrative window;
- how their separate evidence affects the shared confrontation;
- why neither route's exclusive discovery becomes redundant.

## Author-approval risks

- Assigning route-exclusive player actions to named protagonists is an adaptation decision, not direct source truth.
- Exact synchronization of the two routes is unsupported by source dates and should remain flexible.

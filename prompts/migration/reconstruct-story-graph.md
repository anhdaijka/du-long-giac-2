# Reconstruct Story Graph from External Source

Act as a narrative reconstruction analyst, not a novelist yet.

Read:
- `AGENTS.md`
- `.agents/rules/09-source-adaptation.md`
- `docs/playbooks/external-source-ingestion.md`
- the closest source-specific playbook
- the current source inventory

Then:

1. Reconstruct entities, event/task nodes, prerequisites, outcomes, chronology, branch points, flags, dialogue provenance and convergence.
2. Use `templates/migration/source-map.md`, `story-graph.md`, and `branch-matrix.md` where applicable.
3. Label important claims as `DIRECT SOURCE`, `SOURCE-SUPPORTED INFERENCE`, or `UNRESOLVED`.
4. Record contradictions instead of silently choosing a cleaner version.
5. Separate gameplay/implementation mechanics from in-world narrative facts.
6. Identify missing causal/emotional bridges, but do not write them as source facts.
7. Do not initialize or update Story Skills canon.
8. End with the minimum unresolved questions that materially affect adaptation policy.

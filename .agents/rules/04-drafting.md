# Rule 04: Prose Drafting Engine (Bộ Chấp Bút Văn Xuôi)

> **CỔNG CHẶN TIÊN QUYẾT BẮT BUỘC (PRECONDITION HARD STOP)**:
> Tuyệt đối **CẤM** tạo mới hoặc chỉnh sửa tệp bản thảo `chapters/chapter_XX.md` nếu chưa có tệp **`briefs/chapter_XX_brief.md`** được Tác giả phê duyệt rõ ràng. Mọi hành vi viết văn xuôi trước khi duyệt Brief đều là vi phạm nghiêm trọng quy trình State Machine và bản thảo đó sẽ bị vô hiệu hóa!

Inside an approved scene plan, you may creatively realize wording, gesture, micro-actions, sensory detail, rhythm, dialogue, and moment-to-moment texture.

Mandatory Quality Binding:
- MUST strictly comply with `.agents/rules/11-prose-quality-contract.md` (Pure Show Don't Tell, No Explanatory Scaffolds, No AI Cliches, No Head-Hopping, Kim Dung Atmospheric Closures).
- MUST verify all facts and source events directly via `.agents/rules/10-provenance-kernel.md`.
- Target Word Count Band: **4,000 – 4,800 words** per chapter (Hard floor: 3,500 words; Soft ceiling: 5,200 words). If a scene sequence exceeds 5,200 words, split into two connected chapters with natural dramatic beats.

Core Architectural Protocols (The 5 Generalized Invariants):

1. **Spatiotemporal Realism Protocol (Giao thức Hiện thực Không - Thời gian)**:
   - Before drafting any scene involving travel, communication, or external news, consult `worldbuilding/geography/travel_matrix.md`.
   - All physical movement, messenger birds, and rumor propagation must strictly respect terrain and transit duration. Prohibit instantaneous travel and cross-regional telepathy between parallel POV arcs.

2. **Epistemic Horizon & Anti-Omniscience Protocol (Giao thức Ranh giới Tri thức Nhân vật)**:
   - Before drafting, check the POV character's current knowledge ledger in `characters/<character>.md`.
   - The narrative camera (strict third-person limited) can ONLY perceive, interpret, and act upon facts the character has learned in-world. Strictly prohibit authorial omniscience, premature lore drops, or characters reacting to secrets not yet revealed to them.

3. **Martial Progression Compatibility Protocol (Giao thức Tiến trình Võ học & Tương thích Nhân sinh)**:
   - Before drafting combat or training, consult the character's martial progression tier in `characters/<character>.md`.
   - Weaponry, martial stances, inner energy, and combat efficacy must strictly match the character's designated tier for the current Volume/Arc. Martial progression MUST mirror psychological growth and moral crises (Kim Dung principle); never grant premature ultimate skills.

4. **Living Plebeian Texture Protocol (Giao thức Hơi thở Dân sinh & Kháng Game-hóa)**:
   - Ground every chapter in the living socio-economic fabric of its setting: local trades, dialect, market prices (tea, wine, grain), weather, and civilian struggles drawn from the ambient dialogue and linktask corpus.
   - Strictly prohibit "game-like quest running" (characters moving solely between quest triggers and boss encounters without everyday human texture).

5. **Dynamic Pacing & Fluid Volume Scope (Giao thức Nhịp điệu & Mở rộng Linh hoạt)**:
   - Pacing must be low-burn and organic. The chapter outline in `plot/` serves as a dynamic baseline roadmap, NOT a rigid cage.
   - Chapters can dynamically expand in number whenever deeper character arcs, detective investigation, military nuance, or sub-quest resolutions require room to breathe.

Do not silently change:
- approved scene outcome
- established canon
- character knowledge
- physical/world rules
- timeline and geographic locations
- relationship trajectory
- durable object/character state

If a constraint makes the scene fail, stop at the smallest necessary scope and flag the conflict instead of rewriting the architecture.

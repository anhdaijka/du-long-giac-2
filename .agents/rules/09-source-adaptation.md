# Source Adaptation Rule

Use this rule when a project starts from an existing external narrative corpus: game databases, quest scripts, branching dialogue, screenplay material, wiki/lore exports, legacy story bibles, or other pre-existing source material.

## Separation of layers

- **Source corpus** answers: what exists in the source?
- **Reconstructed source model** answers: how do source entities, chronology, branches, flags, dialogue and outcomes relate?
- **Adaptation decisions** answer: what should the novel preserve, transform, merge, omit, reorder or invent as connective tissue?
- **Story Skills canon** answers: what is currently true in the novel?

Never collapse these layers.

## Required order

1. Inventory source material and preserve provenance.
2. Reconstruct narrative structure before adapting it.
3. Classify evidence as direct source, source-supported inference, or proposed novelization bridge.
4. Record contradictions and unresolved source gaps instead of silently resolving them.
5. Propose an adaptation contract and receive author approval.
6. Promote only approved, novel-relevant material into Story Skills canon.
7. Validate Story Skills state after promotion.

## Prohibitions

- Do not treat raw game/database rows as automatic novel canon.
- Do not import every NPC, item, quest or flag into Story Skills just because it exists.
- Do not mistake gameplay mechanics for dramatic causality.
- Do not linearize mutually exclusive branches without recording the chosen branch policy.
- Do not silently invent missing lore to make the source cleaner.
- Do not destroy or overwrite the source corpus while adapting it.

When this mode applies, read `docs/playbooks/external-source-ingestion.md` and then the closest source-specific playbook before canonization.
